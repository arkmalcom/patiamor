# Patiamor Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

This is an English translation of our Spanish privacy policy, which is the original. If the two ever differ, the Spanish version applies.

Patiamor is a free platform for finding dogs and cats for adoption in the Dominican Republic. It connects people who want to adopt with shelters and rescuers. This policy explains what data of yours we keep, why, who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

## Who is responsible for your data

Patiamor (patiamor.com) is responsible for the processing of the personal data we keep. You can write to us at **hola@patiamor.com** with any question or request about your data.

## The essentials

- **We don't sell your data or show ads.** We don't use advertising or tracking tools.
- **A shelter only sees your contact details when you send it a request** for one of its animals, and only while that request is open.
- **We don't keep your exact location.** If you let us use it, your phone sends a point rounded to about 1 km, and we turn it into a district or neighborhood.

## What data we keep and why

**Your account.** Your email address and, if you give them to us, your name, your phone number and how you prefer to be contacted (we remember them from your last request). We use them so you can sign in and so your requests reach the shelters. If you sign in with Google, Google gives us your name and email. We don't receive your Google password or anything else from your Google account.

**When you sign in.** We email you a code that expires after 15 minutes. We keep a record of your session (a cookie and the type of browser you use) so you don't have to sign in every time. A session lasts up to 90 days from the last time you used Patiamor. To prevent abuse, we keep an encrypted fingerprint of your IP address next to each code we send, not the IP itself. Cloudflare Turnstile also checks that whoever asks for a code is a person and not a program.

**Your adoption requests.** When you ask to meet an animal, we keep your answers (space, time, costs), whatever you choose to tell us about your home (housing, other animals, children, experience, when you can visit, a message), how you prefer to be contacted, and how far you are from the animal. When a request ends, we also keep the reason, including the one you give if you withdraw it.

**Emails we send you about your requests.** We email you when you send a request, and again, at most once a day, when a request ends without the animal being yours (the shelter moved on with another family, the animal found a home with someone else, or it is no longer available). If you asked us to tell you when an animal can take requests again, we write to you once. We don't send you advertising.

**Special requests, shelter-owner requests and review requests.** If you ask to adopt more than three animals at once, to register a shelter, or to review the suspension of your shelter, we keep what you write in the form and, where we ask for it, your phone number so we can talk to you. If you register a shelter, we also keep the verification application: contact person, address, tax ID (RNC) if it has one, social media, rescue history, and the details of the references you give us. **If you give us another person's details as a reference, make sure they agree.**

**Notes by the Patiamor team.** If someone at Patiamor verifies an adopter or reviews a request, they write an internal note (for example, how they know that person). Only the Patiamor team sees it. Shelters only see the "Verified by Patiamor" mark («Verificado por Patiamor»), never the note.

**Your preferences on your phone.** Your filters, distance, area and the animals you have already seen are kept in your own browser, not on our servers. You can delete them by clearing the site's data in your browser.

**Errors.** When something fails in Patiamor, we send an error report to Sentry so we can fix it. Before sending it, we remove cookies, the contents of forms, IP addresses and any account data.

## Who we share your data with

- **The shelter or private individual you send a request to.** A private individual is a person who is not a shelter and whom the Patiamor team has met and approved to post; families see them as «Particular» (private individual) and their area, never their name, but they will see you with your details just like a shelter would, and you know this before you send. They see your name, your answers, what you told us about your home and, while the request is open, your email, your phone number and how you prefer to be contacted. You accept this in the form before sending each request. When the request is closed, they stop seeing your email and phone number but keep your answers and the reason it was closed. They contact you through their own means (WhatsApp, a call or email). What you discuss outside Patiamor stays between you.
- **The shelters we put you in touch with,** if you sent us a special request to adopt more than three animals and agreed to share your phone number with them.
- **The services that make Patiamor work,** which process data only to provide that service to us:
  - Cloudflare: servers, database, photos and the check against automated programs.
  - Resend: sending emails.
  - Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  - Sentry: error reports, without personal data.

  These companies may have servers outside the Dominican Republic, for example in the United States.
- **Authorities,** only if the law requires it.

We don't share your data with anyone else, and we never sell it.

## Shelter and animal data

Animal listings (photos, story, health, compatibility, area) are public: that is what they are for. For a shelter we show its name and its area. Photos uploaded by shelters are cleaned before being stored: we remove the hidden data in the file, such as the GPS location. Ninety days after an animal is adopted, we delete all its photos except the main one.

## How long we keep your data

- **Your account and your requests:** while you have an account on Patiamor, or until you ask us to delete them. You see ended requests in «Mi lista» (My list) for 30 days, and the shelter sees them in its inbox for 90 days. After that they stop being shown, but they stay in our records for as long as your account exists.
- **Sessions:** 90 days without using Patiamor.
- **Sign-in codes:** they expire after 15 minutes.
- **Patiamor team decisions** (verifications, pauses, approvals): kept in an internal record for as long as the affected account exists.

## What you can ask us

Under Law 172-13, you can ask us to:
- show you the data we have about you;
- correct it if it is wrong;
- delete it;
- stop using it.

For any of these, write to us at **hola@patiamor.com** from your account's email address. We will answer within a maximum of 10 business days. If we delete your account, your open requests are closed and the shelter stops seeing your data. What a shelter has already noted on its own outside Patiamor is not in our hands.

You can change your name yourself in «Mi cuenta» (My account). Your phone number and how you prefer to be contacted are updated each time you send a request. For any other change, write to us.

## Minors

To adopt on Patiamor you must be over 18. We do not knowingly create accounts for minors. If you believe a minor has given us their data, write to us and we will delete it.

## Security

Connections to Patiamor are encrypted (HTTPS). Codes and sessions are stored encrypted with a one-way algorithm (hash), and access to data is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfect: if something happens that affects your data, we will tell you.

## Changes to this policy

If we change something important, we will let you know on Patiamor before it takes effect. The date above shows the latest version.
