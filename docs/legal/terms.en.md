# Patiamor Terms of Use

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

This is an English translation of our Spanish terms of use, which are the original. If the two ever differ, the Spanish version applies.

These terms explain the rules for using Patiamor (patiamor.com). By creating an account or using Patiamor, you accept them. If you don't agree, don't use Patiamor. For how we handle your data, read the [Privacy Policy](./privacy.en.md).

## What Patiamor is, and what it isn't

Patiamor is a free platform that helps dogs and cats looking for a family in the Dominican Republic find the people who want to adopt them. Shelters and rescuers post their animals, and interested people send them requests.

**Patiamor does not own the animals and does not carry out the adoptions.** Each adoption is agreed between the shelter and the family. The shelter decides who each animal goes to, and on what conditions. Patiamor does not guarantee the health, temperament or information of any animal, nor that a request will end in an adoption.

**Patiamor charges nothing,** neither to adopters nor to shelters.

## Your account

- You must be over 18 to create an account and send requests.
- Use real details: your name, an email, and a phone number where you can really be reached.
- Your account is personal. Don't share it, and tell us if someone got into it without your permission.

## If you want to adopt

- **Send a request only if you truly can and want to give that animal a home.** You can have up to three open requests at a time. If you want to adopt more, there is a special request that the Patiamor team reviews.
- By sending a request, you agree that the shelter sees your answers and your contact details to talk to you about the adoption.
- Withdrawing a request is possible, but it should be rare. If you withdraw three in 90 days, you won't be able to send new requests until we talk to you.
- The "Verified by Patiamor" mark («Verificado por Patiamor») is given by the Patiamor team when it has met or spoken with a person. It is not a right and guarantees nothing, and we can remove it.

## If you post animals (shelters, rescuers and private individuals)

To post, your shelter must be approved and verified by the Patiamor team. If you are not a shelter but a person looking for a family for your animals (for example, a litter), you can ask to post as a **private individual** («particular»): the team gets to know and approves you, not each listing. Families see you as «Particular» and your area, without your name, and you see the contact details of whoever sends you a request while it is open. A private individual cannot ask for any fee or money, and can have up to 10 animals posted at a time. By posting, you commit to:

- **Not selling animals.** Patiamor is only for adoptions. Sales, commercial breeding, "deposits" to reserve an animal, and exchanges for money or goods are not allowed. An adoption fee is acceptable only for shelters, and only if it covers real costs of the animal (vaccines, sterilization, care) and is shown on its listing. The fee can be **up to RD$2,000**. If a shelter needs to charge more (for example, for a surgery it paid for), it must discuss it first with the Patiamor team, which can raise the limit for that shelter.
- Posting only animals that are in your care and at least 8 weeks old.
- Describing each animal honestly: its health, its temperament and who it gets along with, as far as you know.
- Using photos that are yours or that you have permission to use.
- Keeping your animals' status up to date, marking «En proceso» (in process), «Se fue a casa» (went home) or «Retirado» (withdrawn) when appropriate, and answering requests within a reasonable time.
- Using adopters' data only for the adoption they wrote to you about, and not sharing it with anyone else.

By uploading photos and text, you give us permission to show them on Patiamor and in the links shared for your animals, while they are posted and afterwards, as a record of their adoption. The content remains yours.

## What no one may do on Patiamor

- Deceive, impersonate another person or another shelter, or give false information.
- Harass, insult or threaten anyone, inside or outside Patiamor, over something that started here.
- Use Patiamor to sell animals or anything else, or to ask for money by deception.
- Use other people's data for anything other than adoption.
- Try to get into other people's accounts, overload the service or copy data automatically.

## Moderation and suspension

We may review, hide or remove any listing, and pause or suspend any account or shelter that does not follow these terms or that puts animals or people at risk. Where possible, we will tell you the reason, and you can reply by writing to hola@patiamor.com.

If we suspend your shelter, its owner can **request a review** from the shelter portal, explaining why the suspension should be lifted. A person on the Patiamor team will read it and can lift the suspension or keep it. We will answer by email and, if we keep it, we will tell you why. Up to three reviews can be requested in 90 days.

## Liability

We do our best to make Patiamor work well and to verify shelters. But we cannot check everything, and Patiamor is offered "as is".

To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for:
- agreements between shelters and adopters;
- the condition of the animals;
- the conduct of people who meet through Patiamor;
- service interruptions.

Always meet the animal and the person or shelter before an adoption, and if something seems strange to you, let us know.

## Changes

We may change these terms. If the change is important, we will let you know on Patiamor before it applies. If you keep using Patiamor afterwards, you accept the new version.

## Governing law

Patiamor is open to shelters and adopters across the Dominican Republic. These terms are governed by Dominican law. Any dispute will be resolved before the courts of Santo Domingo, Distrito Nacional, unless the law says otherwise.
